How much do sessions cost?
Sessions are a flat £20 per hour — no hidden fees, no registration costs and no minimum commitment. Your first introductory session is completely free.
How long is a session?
Sessions start from one hour and can run longer if that suits — for example before a big exam. We don’t do shorter than an hour, so there’s always time to make real progress.
Is the first session really free?
Yes. The introductory session is free and needs no payment details. If it isn’t the right fit, you pay absolutely nothing and there’s no obligation to continue.
What times are you available?
Flexible around school, including evenings and weekends. Tell me the slots that suit your family and I’ll do my best to fit them — online sessions make this much easier.
Are sessions online or in person?
Both. We prefer online (Zoom or Microsoft Teams), which works brilliantly and fits any schedule. In person, we tutor around Altrincham (e.g. the library), Sale, Timperley and the wider Trafford area.
Which subjects do you cover?
GCSE Maths, Further Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Economics, plus 11+ preparation. The list is deliberately short — I only tutor subjects I scored a grade 9 in myself. I know the AQA specifications best and am confident across the other boards.
Is the Trafford 11+ exam changing?
Yes. For 2027 entry (test sat 14 September 2026) nothing changes — it stays the GL Assessment test covering verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning and maths. From 2028 entry the Trafford consortium moves to a new provider, FSCE, and the test becomes based on the Key Stage 2 English and maths curriculum up to the end of Year 5 — the reasoning papers are dropped entirely. From 2029 entry the test also moves earlier, to the summer term of Year 5. I prepare children for whichever version they'll actually sit — the 11+ page has the full breakdown.
Who will be tutoring my child?
Daniel Stevenson — the founder — personally teaches every session. He’s an AGSB sixth-former who scored a grade 9 (96%) in GCSE Maths a year early, holds OCR Level 3 Further Maths at grade A, and is predicted A*A*A* plus an A at A-Level. You’re never handed off to anyone else. More about Daniel.
What year groups do you teach?
Mainly Years 9–11 for GCSE, and Years 4–5 for 11+ preparation. If your child is in a different year, just ask and we’ll happily advise.
How do I book?
The quickest way is to text or WhatsApp me directly on 07467 284 353 — I’ll answer any questions and arrange your free introductory session. You can also email dmtuition2026@gmail.com, or use the short enquiry form if you’d rather write everything down.
How is payment handled?
Simply by bank transfer, arranged directly once you’ve had your free intro and are happy to continue. No card details are needed to book.
What is your cancellation policy?
Life happens — we just ask for around 24 hours’ notice where possible so we can offer the slot to someone else.
Is there a minimum commitment?
None. Book session by session, or ask us about a regular plan if you’d prefer a consistent weekly slot.
Are you DBS-checked?
Yes — Daniel holds a current DBS check, and as the only tutor he teaches every session himself. He’s always happy to talk safeguarding through, and many families choose online sessions, which parents can sit in on.
