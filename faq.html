Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered

Everything you need to know about sessions, pricing, subjects and booking. Still unsure? Just send us a message.

Book Free Intro Session
How much do sessions cost?
Sessions are a flat £15 per hour — no hidden fees, no registration costs and no minimum commitment. Your first introductory session is completely free.
How long is a session?
Sessions start from one hour and can run longer if that suits — for example before a big exam. We don’t do shorter than an hour, so there’s always time to make real progress.
Is the first session really free?
Yes. The introductory session is free and needs no payment details. If it isn’t the right fit, you pay absolutely nothing and there’s no obligation to continue.
What times are you available?
Almost any time, any day. With the two of us we can usually flex around your family’s schedule, including evenings and weekends — just tell us what suits you.
Are sessions online or in person?
Both. We prefer online (Zoom or Microsoft Teams), which works brilliantly and fits any schedule. In person, we tutor around Altrincham (e.g. the library), Sale, Timperley and the wider Trafford area.
Which subjects do you cover?
GCSE Maths, Further Maths, Physics and Economics, plus 11+ preparation. We're deliberately starting focused — we only tutor the subjects we scored a grade 9 in and study at A-Level. We know the AQA specifications best and are confident across the other boards.
Who are the tutors?
Current Altrincham Grammar School for Boys students with top grades — including a Top 2% British Physics Olympiad placement. You can meet them here.
What year groups do you teach?
Mainly Years 9–11 for GCSE, and Years 4–5 for 11+ preparation. If your child is in a different year, just ask and we’ll happily advise.
How do I book?
Fill in the short enquiry form on our homepage and we’ll be in touch quickly to arrange your free introductory session.
How is payment handled?
Simply by bank transfer, arranged directly once you’ve had your free intro and are happy to continue. No card details are needed to book.
What is your cancellation policy?
Life happens — we just ask for around 24 hours’ notice where possible so we can offer the slot to someone else.
Is there a minimum commitment?
None. Book session by session, or ask us about a regular plan if you’d prefer a consistent weekly slot.
Are you DBS-checked?
Our lead tutor holds a DBS check. We’re always happy to talk through safeguarding, and many families choose online sessions, which parents can sit in on.
