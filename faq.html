Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered

Everything you need to know about sessions, pricing, subjects and booking. Still unsure? Just send us a message.

Book Free Intro Session
How much do sessions cost?
Every session is a flat £15 — there are no hidden fees, registration costs or minimum commitments. Your first introductory session is completely free.
Is the first session really free?
Yes. The introductory session is free and requires no payment details. If it isn't the right fit, you pay absolutely nothing and there's no obligation to continue.
Which subjects do you cover?
Every GCSE subject, plus 11+ preparation. Our tutors specialise in maths and the sciences, with the wider AGSB network covering English, humanities, languages, computer science and more.
Are sessions in person or online?
Both. We tutor in person across Altrincham, Hale, Bowdon, Timperley and Sale, and offer online sessions nationwide.
Who are the tutors?
Current and recent Altrincham Grammar School for Boys students with outstanding results — including 8 grade 9s at GCSE and a Top 2% British Physics Olympiad placement. You can meet them here.
What year groups do you teach?
Primarily Years 9–11 for GCSE, and Years 4–5 for 11+ preparation. Get in touch if your child is in a different year — we'll happily advise.
How do I book?
Fill in the short enquiry form on our homepage and we'll be in touch within a few hours to arrange your free introductory session.
How are payments handled?
Payment is arranged directly and simply after your free intro session, once you're happy to continue. No card details are needed to book.
