Why students lose marks — and how we fix it

Economics rewards clear diagrams and balanced evaluation. Students lose marks by describing rather than analysing, and by skipping the "but on the other hand" that top answers always include.

I take Economics at A-Level and am predicted an A, so I teach the analyse-then-evaluate chains that examiners actually reward — not just the content.
