Why students lose marks — and how we fix it

Languages reward consistent vocabulary, secure grammar and confidence in the speaking exam. Marks are lost on tense accuracy, on the photo-card and role-play, and on translation precision.

We focus on high-frequency vocabulary, the core tenses, and the exam-board structures examiners reward across all four skills.
