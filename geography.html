Why students lose marks — and how we fix it

Geography rewards precise use of case studies and a sharp grasp of command words. Marks are lost when answers are vague, when figures and place names are missing, or when "evaluate" questions don’t reach a judgement.

We help students lock down their case studies, master the mark-scheme command words, and structure the longer answers that carry the most marks.

What we cover

  • Physical: rivers, coasts & natural hazards
  • Ecosystems & climate change
  • Urban issues & challenges
  • The changing economic world
  • Resource management
  • Fieldwork & geographical skills

How we teach it

  • Case studies organised and memorised
  • Command-word technique (describe / explain / evaluate)
  • Map, graph and data skills practised
  • Longer-answer structure drilled
