Why students lose marks — and how we fix it

Maths rewards two things above all: fluency and exam technique. Plenty of students understand a topic in the lesson but lose marks under timed conditions — on method marks, on "show that" questions, on the multi-step problem solvers at the back of the paper.

Both founders scored a grade 9 in Maths and take Maths and Further Maths at A-Level, so they know precisely where the marks hide, how examiners award method marks, and how to recover when a question looks unfamiliar.

What we cover

  • Number, ratio & proportion
  • Algebra, sequences & graphs
  • Geometry & trigonometry
  • Probability & statistics
  • Higher-tier problem solving
  • Non-calculator technique

How we teach it

  • Topic-by-topic diagnosis to find the exact gaps
  • Past-paper drilling under timed conditions
  • Method-mark technique for "show that" and worded questions
  • Foundation and Higher tier, Years 9–11

Every lesson is planned around exactly what you're struggling with — we work through the exam questions you find hardest and teach the technique that turns near-misses into full marks. We only ever tutor the subjects we achieved a grade 9 in.
