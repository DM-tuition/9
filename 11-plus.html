Tutors who have actually sat the 11+

The 11+ is as much about temperament as ability. Bright children often lose marks to time pressure, unfamiliar question styles and nerves. I sat the 11+ myself and won a place at Altrincham Grammar School for Boys — I remember exactly what it takes, and I coach with calm, practical confidence.

We prepare children for the selective and independent schools across Trafford and Greater Manchester, including Altrincham Grammar School for Boys, Altrincham Grammar School for Girls, Sale Grammar, Loreto Grammar, Stretford Grammar and Urmston Grammar, among others.

Verbal Reasoning

Vocabulary, codes, letter and word logic — pattern-spotting practised until it's fast and instinctive.

Non-Verbal Reasoning

Sequences, rotations and spatial logic, with a clear method for the trickier question types.

Maths

Mental arithmetic, problem solving and the worded questions that catch children out under time pressure.

English & Comprehension

Reading inference, grammar, spelling and writing — building the comprehension marks that decide places.

How our 11+ preparation works

  • An honest starting assessment of strengths and gaps
  • Timed practice that builds pace and composure
  • Technique for each question type, not just answer-checking
  • Calm, confidence-first coaching for exam day
  • Online (Zoom / Teams) or in person — and flexible timing to suit your family
