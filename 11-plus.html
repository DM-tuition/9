⚠️ The Trafford 11+ is changing — here's exactly how

In July 2026 the Trafford Grammar Schools Consortium announced its biggest admissions change in years. The test provider, the content and eventually the date are all moving. I prepare children for whichever version of the test they will actually sit.

Unchanged
2027 entry
Test sat 14 September 2026 (Year 6)
Provider: GL Assessment

Two one-hour multiple-choice papers covering verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning and maths. Registration for this cycle closed on 19 June 2026; results follow before 31 October 2026, with school allocations on 1 March 2027.

New format
2028 entry
Test sat September 2027 (Year 6)
Provider: FSCE

Future Stories Community Enterprise takes over. Verbal and non-verbal reasoning papers are dropped. The test is instead built on the Key Stage 2 English and maths curriculum up to the end of Year 5, and is designed to reward applying knowledge rather than reciting it. FSCE does not publish past papers and varies its format year to year.

Date moves
2029 entry onwards
Test moves to summer term of Year 5
Provider: FSCE

The test shifts from September of Year 6 to the latter part of the summer term of Year 5 — several months earlier than now. Preparation that used to run through the summer holiday has to be finished before it.

What the change means for your child

The consortium has been explicit that the new test is meant to sit closer to ordinary classroom learning and reduce the advantage of heavy test-drilling. In practice that shifts what good preparation looks like:

  • Reasoning drills matter less. There is no point grinding through non-verbal reasoning booklets for a test that no longer contains any.
  • Real curriculum mastery matters more. The test rewards children who genuinely understand Key Stage 2 English and maths and can apply it to unfamiliar problems.
  • No past papers to lean on. Because FSCE publishes none, preparation has to build understanding and exam composure rather than memorised question patterns.
  • Timings move forward. For 2029 entry and beyond, a child in Year 4 now has meaningfully less runway than previous cohorts did.

Schools I prepare children for

The Trafford Grammar Schools Consortium sets one test covering all five of its schools — your child sits it once, even when applying to several: Altrincham Grammar School for Boys, Altrincham Grammar School for Girls, Sale Grammar, Stretford Grammar and Urmston Grammar.

Loreto Grammar School is not part of the consortium and runs its own Governors' Entrance Exam, with its own registration directly through the school — so it needs preparing for separately. I also work with children applying to other selective and independent schools across Trafford and Greater Manchester.

Dates and formats above reflect the consortium's announcements as of August 2026. Admissions arrangements do change, so always confirm the current detail on your target school's own admissions pages before relying on it — and ask me if anything is unclear.
